FAQ

Questions, answered.

What exactly do I get for AED 1200?

Full-year access to video lessons and companion notes for your chosen grade and stream. One price covers the academic package — no monthly subscription, no hidden add-ons.

How long do I have access?

Access runs for the full academic year of your package. Watch at your own pace, revisit topics before exams, and keep the notes available throughout.

Do you follow NCERT?

Yes. Lessons follow the CBSE curriculum and NCERT textbooks, taught chapter by chapter at the board so school and home study stay aligned.

Can I pay in Indian rupees?

Yes. Packages are priced at AED 1200 (approximately ₹27,500). Families in India can pay in INR — contact us and we will share the current rupee option.

Is there a free demo?

Yes. Watch a sample lesson and browse sample notes before you buy. Use the demo section above, or message us on WhatsApp for a guided walkthrough.

What if my child falls behind?

Every lesson stays available for the year. Students can rewind board work, rewatch difficult chapters, and catch up without waiting for a live class timetable.
