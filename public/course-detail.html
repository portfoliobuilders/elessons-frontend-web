About this course

What a year with us looks like

Subscribe to the annual package and every Grade 9 CBSE course opens on day one — nothing drips out week by week. Lessons are built around the NCERT textbook chapter by chapter, including the worked exercises, so what a student sees here lines up with what their school teacher is covering.

The teaching style is deliberately plain. A teacher, a board, and the problem being worked through in real time. Families tell us this is the reason they stay: it looks like the classroom their child already knows, so there is nothing new to get used to.

Who this suits

Students who want to read ahead of their school class, students catching up after missing lessons, and students in the Gulf who want CBSE teaching in the Indian style at an Indian price.
