A real teacher at a real board.

Every lesson is taught the way it is taught in a classroom — chalk, board, worked examples, one step at a time. Nothing is animated at you and nothing is rushed. The syllabus follows the CBSE / NCERT sequence exactly, so what you watch on a Tuesday is what the school covers that week.
