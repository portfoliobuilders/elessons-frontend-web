    About this course

    A real teacher at a real board.

    Every lesson is taught the way it is taught in a classroom — chalk, board, worked examples, one step at a time. Nothing is animated at you and nothing is rushed. The syllabus follows the CBSE / NCERT sequence exactly, so what you watch on a Tuesday is what the school covers that week.

    Each chapter comes with a written notes file you can download and print, and the exercises are worked in full rather than left as an answer key. Recorded gives you the whole library on day one to watch at your own pace. Live + Recorded adds weekly scheduled sessions where you can ask questions in real time — and every live session is recorded and added to your library afterwards.

    Terms: access runs for the full academic year from the date of purchase. Fees are non-refundable once the LMS account is activated. Live session timetables are published at the start of each term and may be rescheduled with notice. Lesson counts shown reflect the published syllabus and may be extended during the year.
