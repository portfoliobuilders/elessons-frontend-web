About us

A real teacher at a real board.

G-TEC eLessons.net is the hybrid school arm of G-TEC Education. We teach CBSE / NCERT the way a classroom teaches it — chalk, board, worked examples — for grades 8 to 12, with recorded lessons, mentorship support, and printable notes for every chapter.

Who we serve

Families in India, the UAE, Bahrain, Saudi Arabia and across the Gulf who want board-aligned lessons without giving up the clarity of a traditional class.

What you get

  • Recorded lessons plus mentorship support for the academic year
  • PDF notes per chapter
  • Full packages or single subjects
  • English Grammar complimentary with every annual package
