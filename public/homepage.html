Questions

Before you buy.

What exactly do I get for AED 1,200?
Every video lesson and every notes file for one grade package, for the full academic year. There is nothing else to buy and no chapter is held back.
How long do I have access?
Until the end of the academic year you enrolled for. The whole year unlocks the day you pay, so you can revise or run ahead at any point.
Do you follow NCERT?
Yes. Lessons and notes are mapped chapter by chapter to the NCERT textbooks for the CBSE curriculum, grades 8 to 12.
Can I pay in Indian rupees?
Yes. Prices are listed in AED, and Indian cards, UPI and net banking are charged the equivalent in rupees at checkout.
Is there a free demo?
Yes. A sample lesson and a sample notes page are available above. No sign-up needed to watch.
What if my child falls behind?
Nothing expires mid-year. Any lesson can be rewatched as often as needed, and the notes for that chapter stay downloadable throughout.
