Questions

Before you buy.

Still unsure? Message us on WhatsApp and a real person will answer.

What exactly do I get for the annual fee?
Every video lesson and every notes file for one class, for the full academic year. Bundles run from ₹12,000, and single subjects start at ₹4,000. Grades 8 and 9 are ₹12,000, grade 10 is ₹15,000, and grades 11 and 12 are ₹18,000. There is nothing else to buy and no chapter is held back.
Are the classes live or recorded?
Both, and you do not choose between them. Every class has scheduled live sessions you can attend and ask questions in, and every session is recorded so it can be rewatched at any time until the academic year ends.
How long do I have access?
Until the end of the academic year you enrolled for. The whole year unlocks the day you pay, so you can revise or run ahead at any point.
Do you follow NCERT?
Yes. Lessons and notes are mapped chapter by chapter to the NCERT textbooks for the CBSE curriculum, grades 8 to 12.
How do I pay?
Prices are listed and charged in Indian rupees. UPI, net banking, and Indian debit and credit cards all work at checkout.
Is there a free demo?
Yes. A full sample lesson and a sample notes page are available above, with more in the demo section. No sign-up needed to watch.
What if my child falls behind?
Nothing expires mid-year. Any lesson can be rewatched as often as needed, and the notes for that chapter stay downloadable throughout.
